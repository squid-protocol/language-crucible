### Security Policy
**DO NOT REPORT VULNERABILITIES IN THIS REPOSITORY.**

The Language Crucible is an intentional DevSecOps honeypot and parser stress-test environment. It is deliberately populated with deprecated, vulnerable, and uncompilable code to benchmark the GitGalaxy structural engine. 

**Known Vulnerabilities:** We are already aware of the CVEs, prototype pollutions, and RCE vectors contained within these files. They are here on purpose. We do not accept vulnerability reports, and no bounties will be issued for "discovering" flaws in this codebase.
